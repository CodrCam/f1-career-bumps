# Formula 1 team logos

These season-specific white team marks are served by the official Formula 1
media CDN and are used for identification inside Slipstream's statistical
tables and driver views.

- 2025 source pattern:
  `https://media.formula1.com/image/upload/.../common/f1/2025/{team}/2025{team}logowhite.webp`
